🙏 One Prayer Team • One Fellowship

Enhakkore Prayer Team is one prayer team within Hayole Project Fellowship . We gather together to seek God, pray according to Scripture, encourage one another, and grow in faith.

🎯 Mission & Vision

Our purpose and direction as a prayer team.

🙏

Our Mission

To encourage believers to develop a consistent and meaningful life of prayer, seek God according to His Word, and stand together in prayer for individuals, families, the church and our community.

👁️

Our Vision

To see people become consistent, faithful and Scripture-centered in prayer, building a generation that seeks God and lives according to His Word.

❤️ Why Do We Pray?

Prayer is part of our relationship with God.

❤️

To Know God

Prayer helps us draw near to God and seek Him sincerely.

🕊️

To Seek His Will

Prayer teaches us to depend upon God's wisdom.

🤲

To Intercede

We pray not only for ourselves but also for others.

🛡️

To Stand Firm

Prayer helps us remain spiritually alert and dependent upon God.

📖 Bible Verses & Prayer Lessons

Scripture-based lessons for growing in prayer.

Lesson 1 — Pray Continually

1 Thessalonians 5:17

“Pray without ceasing.”

Prayer should become a continuing part of our relationship with God.

Lesson 2 — Bring Everything Before God

Philippians 4:6–7

Bring your requests before God with prayer and thanksgiving.

Lesson 3 — Pray Sincerely

Matthew 6:6

Jesus teaches His followers to seek God sincerely in prayer.

Lesson 4 — Pray For One Another

James 5:16

Prayer is also a ministry of intercession for others.

Lesson 5 — Ask, Seek and Knock

Matthew 7:7

Jesus encourages His followers to ask, seek and knock.

Lesson 6 — Pray With Alertness

Ephesians 6:18

Believers are called to pray at all times and remain watchful.

🙏 People of Prayer in the Bible

Examples of people who sought God through prayer.

Daniel — Consistent in Prayer

Daniel continued to pray to God even when prayer became dangerous. His life demonstrates consistency and faithfulness in prayer.

Daniel 6:10–23

Jesus — A Life of Prayer

Jesus regularly withdrew to pray and sought the Father's will.

Luke 5:16 • Luke 6:12
🙏

Hannah — Pouring Out Her Heart

Hannah brought her deep pain and desire before God and poured out her heart.

1 Samuel 1:10–18
📖🙏

Paul — Praying For Others

Paul repeatedly prayed for believers and demonstrated intercessory prayer.

Ephesians 1:15–23

⏰ Prayer Alarm

Stand together at your appointed prayer time.

⏰🙏

Be a Member of the Prayer Alarm

Members receive appointed prayer times. When your time arrives, set aside the moment, stop and pray, and faithfully fulfill your prayer commitment.

1️⃣ Receive the prayer time
Pay attention to the prayer time announced by the team.
2️⃣ Set your alarm
Set an alarm on your phone for your appointed prayer time.
3️⃣ Stop and pray
When the alarm rings, take the appointed time to pray.
4️⃣ Be faithful
Honor the prayer commitment you have made and encourage others.
